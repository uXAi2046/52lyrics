# 52lyrics growth work — 8 October 2026

## Audience and baseline

The first audience is English-speaking international readers interested in songs, source editions, and complete texts the site can lawfully display. The local catalog has 13,319 song entries, of which 13,112 are metadata-only, and 108 distinct complete-lyric works. The public site responds over HTTPS and advertises a sitemap in `robots.txt`; a search result for the homepage and individual songs is visible. None of those checks measures total indexing or actual search traffic.

Google Search Console and the site analytics account have not yet been read, so impressions, clicks, indexed URLs, referral visits, and return visits are **unknown**. Do not infer them from a `site:` query or the sitemap size.

## Work prepared in the local source

- Metadata-only song pages use a title promising credits and release notes; pages with a full text say so.
- Two source-led guides cover Tom Lehrer's author documents and the six-stanza *Amazing Grace* text in the 1840 *Olney Hymns* edition. They link to the complete reading pages and primary sources.
- The guide index, homepage cards, sitemap, and pre-render list derive from the shared guide catalog. Complete-text pages link back to the relevant guide.
- The sharing preview now uses a 1200 × 630 PNG generated from the existing vector card, with explicit Open Graph and X image tags.

## Measurement to complete in the site-owner accounts

1. In Search Console, verify the `https://www.52lyrics.com/` property if it is not already verified and submit `https://www.52lyrics.com/sitemap.xml`.
2. Export the last 28 days and previous 28 days from Search performance: query, landing page, country, device, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position. Segment `/guides/`, `/lyrics/`, `/artists/`, and `/albums/`. Inspect important excluded URLs in Page indexing and URL Inspection.
3. In analytics, record sessions by organic search, referral, social, and direct; record landing page and engaged-session rate. Use tagged links below for distribution. Keep the first pre-publication 28-day window as the baseline.
4. Each week, prioritize pages with meaningful impressions and low CTR for title and summary edits, and high-click pages with low engagement for content or navigation fixes. Do not change hundreds of pages based on a single sample.

## First two distribution drafts

**Tom Lehrer source guide**

> Tom Lehrer's song archive publishes the documents behind his lyrics. We matched the words on our reading pages to those source sheets and linked each PDF, so readers can check the edition rather than treating a performance transcript as the only version. Here's how to follow the source for *The Elements* and *Be Prepared*: https://www.52lyrics.com/guides/tom-lehrer-lyrics-source-guide?utm_source=community&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=lehrer-source-guide

**Amazing Grace edition guide**

> The six stanzas of *Amazing Grace* in the 1840 *Olney Hymns* edition appear under the title “Faith's Review and Expectation.” Our guide links the fixed source revision and explains what this edition includes before you compare it with a later performance: https://www.52lyrics.com/guides/amazing-grace-1840-lyrics?utm_source=community&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=amazing-grace-1840

Publish only after the new guide URLs are live. Adapt each draft to the rules and context of the specific community. Replace `utm_source=community` with the actual channel before publishing. Avoid posts whose only purpose is to drop a link.

## Four-week decision point

Compare each guide's search impressions, clicks, and tagged referral sessions after four weeks. If readers reach linked songs and spend time with the source sections, expand the strongest theme into another researched guide. If pages receive impressions but few clicks, refine the search title and summary. If they receive no impressions, inspect indexing and internal links before producing more pages.
