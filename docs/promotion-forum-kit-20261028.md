# Forum promotion kit — 2026-10-28

Ready-to-paste posts for specific communities. Post **manually**, one community at a
time, using an account you own, and keep the affiliation disclosure line — undisclosed
self-promotion gets removed and burns the domain. Adapt the opening line to the thread
you are replying to where noted.

Facts to draw on (verified 2026-10-28): 1,240 recording artists with track-level
MusicBrainz-sourced discographies; 2,206 complete lyric works, each page naming its
printed source edition, revision URL, and rights evidence; 1,145 Commons-licensed
photos with credits; full-text search; nothing is rendered without a pinned source.

## 1. Hacker News — "Show HN"

**Title:** `Show HN: A lyrics site where every text names its printed source and rights evidence`

**Text:**

> I built a lyrics library with a different constraint from the usual lyric sites: a
> song only gets a page if the complete words can be shown with evidence. Every lyric
> page names the exact printed edition it comes from, links the pinned revision of the
> transcription (mostly Wikisource proofread scans), and states the rights basis
> (author death dates, public-domain determinations). 2,206 works are live, from
> Burns' 1887 Poetical Works and Blake's Songs of Innocence and of Experience to
> Dunbar and Whittier. The recording side (1,240 artists) uses MusicBrainz data under
> CC0 with Commons-licensed photos, credited per page.
>
> The interesting engineering problem was the verification pipeline: collectors stage
> into checkpoints that a publish gate re-verifies against the original sources
> (checksums, per-record source re-reads) before anything goes live.
>
> No accounts, no ads, no tracking beyond a visit counter.
>
> https://www.52lyrics.com/lyrics — index of every full text
> Example source page: https://www.52lyrics.com/lyrics/robert-burns-i-burn-i-burn

*Note: post once; respond to comments in the first hours. HN dislikes marketing
adjectives — keep the phrasing factual.*

## What not to do

- 不要用脚本或浏览器自动化批量发帖——违反各站 ToS，且会被判定 spam 连累域名。
- 不要在未登录自己的账号、未披露身份的情况下发。
- 同一篇文案原样贴多个社区（会被交叉识别）；每篇至少改写开头两行。

## Community posting audit (2026-10-28)

An automated pass through the in-app browser checked every target community for an
existing logged-in session that posts could be submitted through:

| Community | Session found | Automated access result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hacker News | No | Rate-limited (`not able to serve your requests this quickly`) |
| V2EX | No | Cloudflare bot challenge |
| Reddit | No | `You've been blocked by network security`; login required |
| Zhihu | No | Redirected to phone/captcha sign-in |
| Douban | Not checked | Same login/captcha class as Zhihu |

None of the communities has a session to post through, and registering accounts
automatically is not possible or permissible: each requires personal credentials
(phone/e-mail verification or captcha), and automated registration/posting violates
their terms. The kit above therefore requires the account owner to log in and post
manually (a few minutes per community), or to log in first and then have the
browser session used to fill and submit a pre-approved draft with a final human
confirmation. Bypassing the bot walls (Cloudflare challenge, captchas) was not
attempted and should not be.
